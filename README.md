# Dhavakveer

**Run. Believe. Achieve. You Got This.**

A marathon & ultra training hub built by a runner, for runners — training plans, a full race history, a daily routine, and a place to share how it's really going.

**Live site:** https://dennysonkusale-png.github.io/dhavakveer/

---

## What's inside

- **Founder's plan** — how I went from a first 10K to a 111 km stadium run, a 100 km trail in the Mahabaleshwar forests and hills, and 50Ks many times over.
- **Race history** — every start line from 2016 to today, on a year-by-year timeline.
- **Daily routine** — my full training day, in English and मराठी.
- **Training plans** — 18 periodised plans across 5K, 10K, 21K, 42K, 50K and ultra; plus 12 sprint plans for 60m, 100m, 200m and 400m. Beginner, intermediate and advanced.
- **Pre- and post-marathon guidance** — taper, fuelling, race morning, recovery, and when to see a professional.
- **Community** — post a workout plan, an experience, or how you feel; share any post to social media along with the plan it relates to.
- **Tools** — pace calculator and a training log.
- **Strava** — follow the actual training behind the plans.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole site — a single self-contained page, no build step, no dependencies. |
| `Dhavakveer-Race-History.xlsx` | The full race record as a spreadsheet: 40 races, with distance, finish time, elevation and notes. |

## Running it locally

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's it — everything is inline.

## Notes

Community posts, the training log and the kit checklist are stored in your own browser via `localStorage`; nothing is uploaded anywhere. The site is general training guidance, not medical advice — consult a doctor before starting a new programme.

The plans draw on my own experience on the road and trail, combined with publicly available research and widely used coaching methodology. Sources are listed on the site.
